## `legoharu` | Narrative and Quest Designer
(he/him/his)
I am an aspiring developer and designer interested in the cross-section between real-life dynamics and digital world applications. My work focuses on the intersection of human social structures and interactive systems. I believe the most immersive game worlds are built on a foundation of realistic social dynamics, power structures, ethical complexity, and a breadth of lore and storytelling.
### My Current "Quests"
* Completing technical post-baccalaureate courses to master C#, Unity, and Data Structures to back my narrative work with technical authority.
* PrimmVitalization, my first coding project; an expansive mod to revitalize the town of Primm within New Vegas, familiarizing myself with the GECK while building economic restoration and faction stratification, turning the town of Primm from forgotten into a hub of commerce and stories.
* More to come
### My Skills & Stats
* My first passion is narrative and quest design; creating branching dialogue, quest architecture, and world-building with the aid of a sociological lens.
* I'm building my technical abilities for now in C#, GECK (Scripting/Quest Tooling), Unity, Git/Version Control.
* Academic background with a Bachelor of Arts in Sociology, hoping to leverage a pathway into Interactive Entertainment and Human-Computer Interactions
* Trilingual ability in English, Portuguese, and Spanish
